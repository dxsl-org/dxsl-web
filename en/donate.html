Where Do Your Contributions Go?

Every dollar received is rigorously accounted for and dedicated 100% toward open engineering research and public infrastructure.

Build Servers & Bandwidth

Sustaining distributed automated CI/CD build runners, package mirror servers, and high-speed distribution pipelines for millions of downloads across Buttre, Cellos, and Haily.

Hardware R&D Testbenches

Procuring evaluation microcontrollers, open-architecture RISC-V compute nodes, ARM SBCs, and peripheral testing units to ensure Cellos OS boots reliably on next-gen silicon.

Digital Transformation for Civic Good

Funding training sessions, open-source deployment toolkits, and digitizing resources for schools, civic archives, and community organizations with zero proprietary software fees.

Statement of Non-Profit Independence

1. Strict Non-Profit Operation: All funds contributed by the community are strictly allocated to engineering infrastructure, testing hardware, and operational costs. There are zero shareholder dividends or executive bonuses.

2. No Data Monetization or Ad Insertion: Buttre IME, Cellos OS, and Haily AI will never bundle telemetry spyware, third-party analytics trackers, or display intrusive ads. Privacy is non-negotiable.

3. Perpetual Open Access (FOSS): Regardless of financial status or future scale, all research output, codebases, and architectural blueprints produced by DXSL remain forever open under OSI-approved licenses.
